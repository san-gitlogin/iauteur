# Craft Patterns — verifiably observed in 9 reference frames (Cloud Codes style)
Apply to EVERY spec; these are the professionalism patterns the operator selected as the channel bar.

1. ONE headline per frame: white, single [accent] phrase = the emotional verdict. Never two accents, never colored whole headlines.
2. SOURCE footer on every content frame — the credibility strip is part of the look, ≤64 chars, factual.
3. Middle-band composition: content occupies the vertical center; top = headline; bottom = source. Air everywhere; nothing touches edges.
4. Two-tier bullets: bold short lead + muted one-line detail (`items[{text, detail}]`). The lead is scannable muted-audio; the detail rewards attention.
5. Proof-by-recognition: attach abstract numbers to FAMILIAR logos (`STAT_CALLOUT.logos`) — "8.4B ops" lands because YouTube/Netflix/Chrome icons sit under it. Dark themes render logos as white mono glyphs.
6. Honesty rows: when comparing, include the row that beats you ("Opus 4.8 — still ahead"). One humility beat per video (a failed prediction, a caveat) BUILDS trust; suppressing it reads as advertising.
7. Person frames: real photo (img: asset, license law) in an accent-bordered card, name in display font + role as mono kicker; facts as two-tier bullets beside, not inside, the photo.
8. Numbers auto-compact (8.4B not 8,400,000,000); mono tabular digits; direction/context word beside the number, never inside it.
9. Channel card close: logo glow ring → name → handle → SUBSCRIBED chip → tagline pill. The subscribe ask is a designed MOMENT, not a plea.
10. Thumbnail: ≤4 words as a fragment, one focal element, badge, readable at 120px. Contrast rule: dark video → one light thumbnail variant and vice versa.
