What's the video about?

Give it a topic. Paste source material if the facts must come from a specific article (leave blank for evergreen/conceptual topics).

Step 2

Pick the look & format

Choose a design pack (this sets the theme) and the video's shape. Match the mood to the topic.

Format

Design pack

Step 3

Author the spec

The console builds the prompt; your LLM writes the scenes; the console validates and saves them. Nothing here calls AI — you paste to and from your own chat.

Regenerate the prompt so the spec matches your new settings, or discard the draft and start over.
