How iAuteur works — 60-second orientation

Two ways to make a video. Manual: iAuteur writes carefully-built prompts that you paste into your own chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…), then paste the answer back and it validates, previews and renders. Automatic: connect your own AI key in Step 3 and it runs that whole loop for you, stopping before render. Either way you stay in control of the model and the facts.

  1. Topic — say what the video is about.
  2. Design — pick a look; it sets the theme for every scene.
  3. Author — paste prompts to/from your LLM, or connect an AI key to automate it.
  4. Voiceover (optional) — pick a voice; narration is synced to the script.
  5. Render — produce the 16:9 or 9:16 video.

Tip: renders and previews take a minute or two — watch the progress bar, it isn't stuck.

Step 3

Author the spec

Two ways to author: connect your own AI below to run the whole flow automatically, or use the manual two-paste flow — the console builds each prompt, your LLM writes the scenes, and the console validates and saves them. Either way, the linter is the judge.
